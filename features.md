# Reel Goblin: Features (all in v1)

## Asked for
- Floating cute goblin over Instagram + YouTube, draggable, remembers position
- Counter on his belly
- Level 1 manual (tap) and Level 2 automatic (self-counting)
- Growth in 6 stages (mouth changes: smile, little "o", grin, roar) + funny lines
- Dashboard: today, time lost, goal bar, 7-day chart with total/average
- **Monster mode**: grows and covers the Reels screen as the count rises
- Survives restart, fully offline, no login

## Extras added (so you do not wait for v2)
- Daily goal: goblin turns angry-red and grumpy when you pass it
- Undo: long-press = -1; dashboard has +1 / -1 fix buttons
- Giant-mode escape hatches: tap = go Home ("touch grass"), long-press = 60 s peek
- Adjustable cover speed (fully covers at 10 to 150 reels)
- Real measured time inside Reels, not only a guess
- Haptic buzz every 10 reels and at your goal
- 3 goblin colours (Mint, Berry, Peach)
- Blinking, bobbing, pop animation on every count

## Ideas to discuss (not built)
- TikTok / Facebook Reels / Snapchat Spotlight support
- Lock-out: goblin blocks Reels until you do a task
- Home-screen widget with today's count
- Weekly "goblin report" image to share
- Optional Gmail-based backup (only if you want it; default stays zero sign-up)
